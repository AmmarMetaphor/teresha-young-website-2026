About

Teresha Young

I'm a Relationship & Emotional Well-being Expert, working with organisations, private clients and the media on the relationships and emotional well-being that shape how we feel, work and live.

Credentials

Accreditation & recognition

  • I'm IAPC&M-accredited at Master Coach level.
  • I serve as Appointed Head of Mental Health & Well-being for IAPC&M members.
  • I've been recognised across a number of industry awards between 2024 and 2026.

Philosophy

I believe healthier relationships change how people feel, work and live — and that real change starts with communication, self-awareness and emotional well-being, whether that's inside a workplace or in someone's personal life.

That belief shapes everything I do, from my work with organisations to private coaching and the conversations I contribute to publicly.
