Outcomes

What this means for your organisation

Healthy workplaces depend on more than policies — they depend on how people communicate, regulate emotion, navigate pressure and relate to one another. When I work with organisations, this is what I focus on:

  • Emotional wellness and resilience
  • Relationships at work
  • Self-awareness
  • Stress management
  • Healthy communication
  • Psychological safety
  • Boundaries
  • Burnout prevention
  • Emotional regulation
  • Confidence
  • Personal effectiveness

Credibility

I'm trusted by organisations including Channel 4 and Mind. I serve as Appointed Head of Mental Health & Well-being for IAPC&M members, and I'm accredited by the IAPC&M at Master Coach level.
