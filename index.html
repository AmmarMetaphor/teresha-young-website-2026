For organisations

What healthy workplaces actually depend on

Healthy workplaces depend on more than policies. They depend on how people communicate, regulate emotion, navigate pressure, establish boundaries and relate to one another.

I work alongside HR, People and wellbeing teams to help organisations recognise and address these dynamics directly — not as an abstract wellbeing add-on, but as a practical part of how a workplace functions.
